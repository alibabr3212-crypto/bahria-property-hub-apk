# Bahria Property Hub

Bahria Property Hub is a free Android app for property leads in Bahria Town Karachi, Pakistan. Property dealers, owners, buyers and tenants can find listings and requirements in one place.

Website: https://bahriapropertyhub.com/

Privacy policy: https://bahriapropertyhub.com/privacy-policy

## What you can do

- Browse leads for sale and for rent: villas, apartments, plots and more
- Switch to Looking For to see what buyers and tenants need
- Search by title, precinct or location
- Filter by sale or rent, property type, precinct, locality, date and price
- Post your own property or requirement
- Save leads to your lists and review them later
- Get notifications and chat inside the app

Prices are shown in PKR and plot sizes in square yards. Sign in with your Google account. Joining is free and no card is required.

## How to install

1. Open the download link on your Android phone and tap Download.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from this browser. The app comes from outside Google Play, so this question is normal.
3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
