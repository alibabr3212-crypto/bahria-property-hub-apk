Bahria Property Hub

Android app for property leads in Bahria Town Karachi

Bahria Property Hub brings property leads for Bahria Town Karachi into one app. Dealers, owners, buyers and tenants can see what is for sale or rent, and what people are looking for, without searching through scattered groups.

Download the APK (21.4 MB)

Version 1.0.0 for 64-bit Android phones. Also see the release page.

What the app does

  • Browse leads for sale and for rent: villas, apartments, plots and more.
  • Switch to Looking For to see what buyers and tenants need.
  • Search by title, precinct or location, and filter by sale or rent, property type, precinct, locality, date and price.
  • Post your own property or requirement, and save leads to your lists.
  • Get notifications and chat inside the app.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. Joining is free, no card is required, and the free plan includes 10 unlocks of seller or requester contact details for 30 days.

How do I sign in?

With your Google account.

Why is the app not on Google Play?

This first version is shared as an APK file. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser. That question is normal for apps installed outside Google Play.

How do I install it?

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this browser.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.

What currency and units are used?

Prices are shown in PKR and plot sizes in square yards.
